- notes: |
    Two different causes, both outside the proxy and both fixable by the user.
    
    优选域名 (66, all dead): every node sets ech-opts.enable=true with
    query-server-name cloudflare-ech.com -- these are Cloudflare ECH hostnames.
    The TLS handshake fails ONLY when SNI is the node's own servername, and
    succeeds on the same IP with a different SNI. That is the signature of an
    ECH-required hostname the client cannot complete. 9 of the 22 unique
    hostnames do not even resolve. mihomo reports 504 (timeout) per node.
    
    IPv6优选 (30, all dead): every server is an IPv6 literal, and the user's own
    Clash Verge script sets config.ipv6=false (mihomo reports ipv6=False).
    Independently, the host cannot reach public IPv6 either -- even Cloudflare's
    own 2606:4700:4700::1111 times out from the OS, so local IPv6 egress is
    broken as well. mihomo reports 503 (connection error, not timeout).
    
    Note: this investigation did NOT change the live mihomo ipv6 flag -- the
    production proxy shares that path, and the user's script disabled IPv6
    deliberately ("防止本地运营商 IPv6 抢跑泄漏"). The finding is reported, not
    acted on.
